# Business Insights

## Netflix Content Analysis

### Key Findings

- Movies represent a larger share of the Netflix content analyzed.
- The dashboard shows the distribution of content across different age categories.
- Content categories can be compared using average release year.
- Release-year analysis helps identify changes in Netflix's content distribution over time.
- The dashboard provides interactive filtering by content type and location.

## Business Recommendations

- Analyze content categories with strong representation to understand content strategy.
- Monitor changes in content production and release trends.
- Compare Movie and TV Show availability across locations.
- Use demographic and category-level analysis to support content planning.
